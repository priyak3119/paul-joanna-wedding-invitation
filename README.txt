CHRISTIAN WEDDING INVITATION TEMPLATE

Upload every file and folder in this package to public_html in Hostinger or cPanel.
No installation, build command, framework, database, or server is required.

QUICK CUSTOMISATION
1. Open index.html in a text editor and replace the fictional demo names, parents, verse,
   dates, church, reception venue, map links, WhatsApp number, and phone number.
   The published demo intentionally contains no real phone number or address.
2. Open script.js and change WEDDING_DATE to the correct date and time.
3. Replace the demo media inside assets while keeping these filenames:
   - invitation-envelope.webp
   - couple-art.webp
   - bride-art.webp
   - bible-rings-art.webp
   - demo-wedding-film.mp4
   - background-music.mp3
4. If you use different filenames, update the matching paths in index.html.

MEDIA RECOMMENDATIONS
- Hero/couple artwork: portrait WebP, around 1200 x 1800 pixels.
- Bride artwork: portrait WebP, around 1200 x 1800 pixels.
- Rings/Bible artwork: landscape WebP, around 1800 x 1200 pixels.
- Video: MP4 (H.264), 720p or 1080p, preferably under 20 MB for fast loading.
- Music: MP3, gently compressed and licensed for your use.

INCLUDED INVITATION FLOW
- You’re Invited envelope opening screen
- Clear scratch-to-reveal Save the Date; the days-only countdown appears after the reveal
- Christian Bible verse, family introduction, and four-event schedule
- Couple story, photo gallery, embedded demo video, and outfit planner
- Conditional RSVP: Attending shows guest/event fields; Wishes Online shows a blessing field
- Visible WhatsApp contacts and RSVP submission without a backend
- Church and venue cards with Google Maps directions
- Floating background-music control
- Energetic opening flower shower that settles into drifting petals, butterflies, and animated church bells

FILES
- index.html: all invitation content and sections
- style.css: colours, spacing, responsive design, and animation
- script.js: opening, scratch reveal, countdown, RSVP, gallery, music, and effects
- assets/: original wedding illustrations, envelope artwork, video, and music

The included names, addresses, dates, phone numbers, and locations are fictional
demo content and must be replaced before publishing the real invitation.
